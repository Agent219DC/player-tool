# Roster Lab repo notes (repo Agent219DC/roster-lab, live at agent219dc.github.io/roster-lab)

- `index.html` is the entire app: the shared Roster Lab page (formerly Player Tool) plus a Firebase shim (sign-in gate, `window.claude.use()` stand-in for db/user/downloads). Edit it directly.
- Sign-in is Google only on the site: the email-link controls on the gate are hidden (Firebase has email-link sign-in off). The owner chose to leave the Firebase Email/Password provider on and `localhost` in authorized domains for now. The browser API key is restricted to agent219dc.github.io and the firebaseapp.com domain.
- Owner email is set in two places: `OWNER_EMAIL` in index.html and `isOwner()` in firestore.rules. Keep them in sync.
- Staff emails live in Firestore at `config/staff` (`emails` array), managed from Owner tools → Staff access.
- Never commit player data or backup JSON files; this repo is public.
- After changing `firestore.rules`, tell the owner to paste it into Firebase → Firestore → Rules → Publish. Rules lock-down: staff may only touch the listed boards (`isBoard`), may write `shared/season` only, and cannot read `config/staff`, `config/market`, `config/backup` or `config/rollover`; any other collection (old `sbc_players`) is owner-read only; permanent player deletes are owner-only. Market price ranges on player cards are owner-only (`watchMarket` runs for the owner).
- Pages are tabs (`data-page` on each section, `go(tab)` router, `#tab` in the URL). Four sides (`tabSide(t)`, body classes `mode-hs` / `mode-an` / `mode-gm`): Portal blue (boards, bigboard, recruit "Roster needs", pvisits "Visits"), High school purple (hsboard, hsbig, hsrec "Visits"), Analytics green for all staff (sbc, overview "Starters vs. targets"), GM gold owner-only (depth, budget, settings; staff still read `shared/depth` for starters). `GM_TABS`/`AN_TABS` decide the side; `TAB_ALIAS` sends old `#hsset` to settings and `#hsneeds` to hsboard (the Class needs page is out of the menus; its data in `config/hs.needs` still feeds the HS boards). The Settings page shows the portal settings and then the HS settings section (`#hsset`, `data-page="settings"`, CSS `order:1`). Portal Visits and HS Visits are separate calendars. Navigation has no hidden sub-views: computers (≥900px) get the `#sidenav` side menu (GM group `#navGm` owner only) plus a `#crumb` line; phones and tablets get the side switch and one tab per page of the current side. `paintNav`/`goSoon` change colors on the click; `navSync` keeps the menu, crumb and title in step. Player and prospect cards use collapsible `details.pc-sec` sections (summary + one-line `.sec-pv` preview) in `.pc-cols` (two columns at 960px+). Owner-only data (budget, asking prices) lives in Firestore `owner/*`, which the rules restrict to the owner; never put money in `config/`, `shared/` or player docs.
- Depth charts live in `shared/depth` (this season) and `shared/depth_next`; saves send only changed spots (`groups.<spot>`). Starters on Overview and the Big Board come from each spot's depth chart #1.
- Branding: the Roster Lab mark is a football with liquid split vertically blue | purple | green (equal amounts) on the sign-in logo and favicon/app icons; in the header the liquid is one solid fill in the current side color (`.rl-liq`, `var(--accent)`) (`icon-*.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `favicon.png`). Side colors: Portal blue `#1fa3ee`, High School purple `#a93fd9`, GM green `#2fcb86`, set as `--accent*` on `body` / `body.mode-hs` / `body.mode-gm`; neutrals are slate grays (no Marshall green). Grade tiers run cool to warm (owner pick, readable in light and dark): Elite indigo #4338ca / #a5b4fc, Impact Starter cyan #0e7490 / #67e8f9, Starter lime #4d7c0f / #a3e635, Rotation amber #b45309 / #fbbf24, NGE red #b91c1c / #f87171 (light / dark) (`--tElite` etc., `TIERRGB` for PDFs). The Marshall logo sits beside the Roster Lab mark (owner approved): header (phones/tablets), bottom of the side menu as a crest (`.sn-foot`; `.mulogo` light/dark ESPN 276 images; never on the sign-in page, owner rule) and every PDF header (`MU_PNG` via `logoData("276")`, left of the Roster Lab mark); it also shows as a school logo. Never use it for the favicon/app icons or outside the site without asking the owner. Confidence flags (High) and good numbers use `--cHigh` green, separate from the lime Starter tier. Every PDF gets `pdfBrand` (via `pdfHeader`): a top bar in the current side color plus the three-color logo (`RL_PNG`, drawn from SVG at load) and ROSTER LAB top right; titles drop the "Roster Lab · " prefix.
- Player cards show ESPN headshots (`headshotHtml`, initials fallback) for players with an `espnId`.
- Player cards load ESPN live on open (`fillEspnLive`): season line, game log, and totals vs. FBS opponents (FBS team ids from ESPN's core API, cached in localStorage). Nothing from it is saved to Firestore.
- School logos come from ESPN team search (`teamDrain`, cached per browser); ESPN's full team list is blocked by CORS.
- Coach notes live on each player doc as `notes[]` ({id,t,at,by,byName}); staff can add, authors (and the owner) can delete. Form saves carry `notes` over (`withTrend`), so editing a player never drops them.
- High School side: the header's Portal / High School switch flips the tab row. HS tabs are `hsboard`, `hsbig`, `hsset` (the `HS` module near the end of index.html). Prospects live in Firestore `hs_players` (covered by the existing `*_players` rule); HS settings live in `config/hs` (owner writes). Film grades are stored per coach on each prospect as `film.<uid>`. Prospect import files (.json) are loaded from HS Settings and must never be committed here.
- HS extras: Prospect board order is `hsRank` on each prospect; `star` (same field and ☆/★ look as the portal) and coach `notes[]` live on the prospect too (an old string `notes` shows as a "Profile note"). Imports never overwrite hsRank, star, ignoreTests, hudl, notes or film. `hudl` is the staff-set Hudl link (`hudlOf` falls back to an imported Hudl `filmUrl`). HS Needs (`hsneeds` tab) reads/writes `config/hs.needs` as `{<class>:{QB:n,...}}`, falling back to `NEED_DEF`. The Prospects list uses portal-style row checkboxes for Compare selected (2–4) and Print selected. `ignoreTests` (array of test keys) on a prospect is a staff call to not count a test; imports never overwrite it. The Settings backup includes `hs_players` and `config/hs`. The header search covers portal and HS (`HS.search`, `HS.open`).
- HS testing weights are per position per test (`testWt`, editable in HS Settings → Testing weights). 10-yd split marks are 0.364 × the 40 marks. Each result counts by reliability (`relOf`: laser/event 1, hand .85, GPS .7, listed .4; 100m-for-40 ×.6; older than 18 months ×.6). Testing's share of the score = (100 − film share) × min(1, coverage/0.6), so thin or shaky testing hands weight to film. For testing-only prospects, coverage under 50% is filled with the Tracking Football estimate (or 70). DE and TE 40 marks are estimates (DE = DT/LB midpoint, TE = WR/OL midpoint). A 100m stands in for a missing 40 or a position with no 40 benchmark.
- HS testing score: linear through each benchmark's 70 and 100 marks, eased above 90 (`ease`) so the 100 mark lands near 95 and nobody ties at 100. CHECK (testing vs. Tracking Football) needs 2+ counted tests.
- HS statuses include `Signed` (`isCom` = Committed or Signed). Realistic targets (`isTgt`) exclude committed elsewhere, Pass and anyone with a P4 offer; the Prospect board, Big Board, Needs targets, film queue and HS Recruiting use only commits + realistic targets. Recruiting data on a prospect: `rec` {recruiter,posCoach,offerDate} and `visits[]` {type,date,note}; imports never overwrite them. HS Recruiting tab is `hsrec`. The portal depth chart's Next season view has "Add HS commits" (next season's class, by name, tagged `t:"fr"` with `hs:<id>`), and portal Recruiting counts HS commits not on that chart in Committed/Left.
- GM side (owner only, gold `#f2b705`): owner rule: anything on the GM side is visible only to the owner. The GM switch button and side-menu group show only for the owner; staff can still read `shared/depth*` (starters on Starters vs. targets and the Big board come from it) but the rules let only the owner write those docs; GM tabs are `depth`, `budget` and `settings` (`okTab` sends anyone else to Boards). The Sun Belt snapshot (`sbc`) is on the Analytics side for all staff. Sun Belt data lives in `analytics/sbc` (2026) and `analytics/sbc2025` ({teams, players[], portal[], dataAsOf, recAsOf}; no money), which all staff read and only the owner writes. Team roster budgets ($M) are money and stay owner-only in `owner/sbc` (`teams.<fid>.budget`, budgetAsOf, budgetSrc); the page merges them in for the owner only, and only the owner sees the Roster budget view, Edit records/budgets and Update portal odds. `owner/sbc` / `owner/sbc2025` still hold the older full copies; load falls back to them for the owner if the analytics doc is missing. `owner/sbcArchive` holds unused old copies (pre-rebuild 2026 class, old stat-line evals); nothing reads it. The Sun Belt page has a 2025 / 2026 season switch (`SBC_YR`): 2026 reads `analytics/sbc`, 2025 reads `analytics/sbc2025` (final full-season two-deep + records + 2025 portal class, Texas State in, Louisiana Tech out; no budget or next-season views). Both two-deeps are position-locked: each unit gets QB 1, RB 1, WR 3 + TE 1, OT 2 + IOL 3, DE 2 + DT 2 + LB 2, CB 2 + S 2 + a fifth DB (nickel if a CB), starters then backups by snaps at that PFF position. Staff position calls override PFF (`posNote` on the player). Portal-class evals are built on the page (`sbcEvalHtml`): strengths/weaknesses from trait codes on each transfer (`tn` = this season, `tp` = last season before the move; top/bottom quarter vs. Sun Belt transfers at his position, phrases in `SBC_TR`), then why he's a good/bad take; no stat lines (the card already has them). The 2025 class also shows each player's portal odds vs. what happened and a "Did the portal odds hold up?" table (`sbcOddsReview`).
- Portal odds: `config/portalOdds` ({bench, cells{"P4|Backup":{n,good,ok,bad,np,avg,lo,hi}}, season, asOf}) holds league-wide base rates only, no player rows (owner decision: built from the 2026 class only while the season is in progress; revisit once 2026 is final), and the owner saves it from Sun Belt → "Update portal odds" (`sbcOddsCalc`). Portal cards show it (`oddsHtml`) because a transfer's board grade barely predicts his grade after a move. In the Sun Belt next-season view, our Committed portal players join at their profile's typical grade (`SBC.inc`). Returning starters keep their spots; returning backups and commits then compete on grade.
