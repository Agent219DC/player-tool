High School Prospects

Score: film grades and testing blended by position, 70 = a Marshall starter. A prospect gets an overall grade once a coach grades his film; until then only his testing score shows. A ~ testing score is estimated from Tracking Football because he has no usable testing; CHECK means our testing and theirs disagree by 25+ points. vs MU is his Tracking Football athletic rating percentile among Marshall players at his position. Confidence is High with film from 2+ coaches and verified testing, Medium with some of each, Low otherwise. Medium is capped at a B, Low at a C. Tap a prospect for his card.

HS Settings

Film traits and blend

Four traits per position in order of importance, graded 1–10 (7 = Marshall starter trait). Film share is how much of the score comes from film; the rest is testing.

Testing weights by position

How much each test counts toward a position's testing score (each row adds up to 100). Linemen lean on the 10-yd split, shuttle and broad jump; receivers and DBs lean on the 40. A 100m stands in for a missing 40. If a prospect's tests cover less than half of his position's weight, the gap is filled with his Tracking Football estimate (or a neutral 70), so one test can't carry his score.

Testing benchmarks

For each position group: the result that scores 70 (current Marshall roster median) and the roster's best (the 100 mark), from Tracking Football's high school data. Scores ease off above 90: matching the roster best lands near 95, and only results well past it approach 100, so top testers keep their order. Small samples are widened to a minimum spread so one great tester can't break the scale.

Grade cutoffs

Below the Rotation cutoff is NGE. Letter grades use the same A–F scale as the portal side.
