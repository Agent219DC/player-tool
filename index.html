Overview

Next season: players marked graduating (0 years left) drop off, every returning player counts whatever his confidence, and every target marked Committed joins the roster and competes for the spots. Remaining targets are paired with the new weakest starters.

One card per starting spot. Each spot shows your current-season players marked On Roster, best first, and your weakest starter is paired with the best target. Only players with Medium or High confidence are listed; if that leaves a spot blank, the listed starter from model settings fills it. Targets exclude On Roster, our own school, hidden, NIP and Pass players. Need: Upgrade when the spot is empty or its player grades below 70 (C−), Solid at 70–79, Great at 80+. Tap a card to open that position (on phones, a row).

Full backup

Download everything (every position's players, Trash, model settings and season info) as one backup file, or restore from one. Restoring adds or replaces players by their ID; it never deletes anyone.

Start a new season

Run this once when the season turns over. At every position it: takes players marked graduating (0 years left) off the roster and hides them, moves every Committed player to On Roster, takes one year off everyone's eligibility, and moves the default season forward. Export all positions first if you want a backup; you can also undo it here.
